# Dependencies and reusable parts

- Even Hub SDK / CLI: installed through npm; their own package licenses apply.
- ws, Vite, TypeScript, tsup, Vitest: installed through npm; their own package licenses apply.
- BlackHole: separately installed by the user; it is not redistributed here.
- Apple frameworks: supplied by the user's macOS/Xcode installation.
- Character: procedural round avatar and animation code; no downloaded sprite packs or reference images bundled.
